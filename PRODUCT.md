# Product

## Register

product

## Users

Windows operators who own the source content and destination accounts. They configure one Douyin source and monitor unattended restreaming to their own Douyin and WeChat Channels destinations, often for long-running sessions where failures must be visible and recoverable.

## Product Purpose

Restream Studio turns one authorized Douyin live-room link into two independently supervised RTMP outputs. Success means the operator can configure, test, start, observe, recover, and stop the full workflow from one local interface without exposing stream credentials or mistaking a queued action for a working stream.

## Brand Personality

Calm, precise, dependable. The interface should feel like a focused broadcast control surface: clear under pressure, restrained in normal operation, and explicit when action is required.

## Anti-references

Not a decorative creator landing page, neon gaming overlay, generic glass-card dashboard, or dense infrastructure console. Avoid fake success states, decorative charts, excessive gradients, nested cards, tiny low-contrast labels, and controls whose effect is unclear.

## Design Principles

1. Show verified state, not optimistic intent.
2. Keep source, Douyin output, and WeChat output operationally distinct.
3. Put the next corrective action beside the failure that requires it.
4. Preserve operator focus during long sessions with stable layout and restrained motion.
5. Make credentials and sensitive URLs invisible by default.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

Target WCAG 2.2 AA. All operations must be keyboard accessible, status must never rely on color alone, focus indicators must remain visible, body text must meet 4.5:1 contrast, and motion must honor reduced-motion preferences. Chinese is the primary UI language; labels and error messages should remain concise and unambiguous.
